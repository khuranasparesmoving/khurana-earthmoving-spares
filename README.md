<h2>Introduction to Khurana Earthmoving Spares (KES)</h2>
<p>Khurana Earthmoving Spares (KES) is an India-based manufacturer, supplier and exporter of replacement spare parts for concrete infrastructure and construction equipment. Established in 2016 in New Delhi, India, the company focuses on supplying components for concrete pumps, transit mixers, concrete batching plants, boom placers and cement silos.</p>
<p>Operating under the philosophy <strong>&ldquo;Engineering Reliability in Concrete Infrastructure,&rdquo;</strong> KES aims to support construction companies, infrastructure developers, equipment operators, concrete contractors and industrial buyers with a wide range of replacement spare parts.</p>
<p>Construction equipment works in demanding conditions involving continuous operation, heavy loads, abrasive concrete materials and challenging site environments. Selecting suitable replacement components is therefore important for equipment maintenance, operational reliability and long-term productivity.</p>
<p>KES focuses on serving this requirement by offering a range of industrial spare parts for concrete production, transportation, pumping and storage equipment.</p>
<h2>About Our Business</h2>
<p>Khurana Earthmoving Spares started its journey in 2016 in New Delhi and has developed its business around the requirements of the concrete construction and infrastructure industry.</p>
<p>The company focuses on providing replacement components that offer buyers an alternative between expensive original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts and low-quality market alternatives.</p>
<p>KES's product approach includes components made using materials and technologies such as alloy steels, tungsten carbide hardfacing, polyurethane and hydraulic sealing solutions, wherever applicable to the relevant product.</p>
<p>Our objective is to help customers identify suitable spare parts for their equipment requirements through a broad product range and customer-focused service.</p>
